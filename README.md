### 2. Core Mission & Value Proposition
Veridion Studio was established to help property owners and hoteliers **break free from over-reliance on 15%–20% Online Travel Agency (OTA) commissions** (such as Airbnb, Agoda, and Booking.com). 

By engineering proprietary, high-speed digital infrastructure, Veridion Studio empowers properties to:
1. **Retain 100% of Booking Revenue:** Direct guest payments flow directly into the property owner’s bank account via integrated payment gateways (QRIS, Credit Card, Virtual Account) with zero middleman commission deductions.
2. **Eliminate Overbooking Risks:** Automated 2-way iCal multi-channel calendar synchronization keeps dates aligned across direct channels and third-party OTAs.
3. **Streamline Daily Front-Desk & Housekeeping Operations:** Lightweight, cloud-based interfaces accessible via regular smartphones or tablets without requiring expensive specialized hardware.
4. **Own Guest Data (Direct CRM):** Full ownership of guest contact details for direct marketing and loyalty repeat-bookings.
